    Khao Sok Safari (Bathing & Canoe or Bamboo)

    Khao Sok National Park is in Suratthani province, Thailand. Its area is 739 sq.km, and it includes the 165 square kilometer Cheow Lan Lake contained by the Ratchaprapha Dam. The park is the largest area of virgin forest in southern Thailand and is a remnant of rain forest which is older and more diverse than the Amazon rain forest.

    Itinerary

    Start 8:30 - 16:00
    •   Transfer from hotel to Khao Sok National Park.
    •   Take in the sights, sound and smells of a morning in the life of the local at Takuapa market.
    •   Grab your camera and get ready to pose with lush green rainforest background at The Khao Sok National Park Viewpoint
    •   Take you to visit a Phanthurat Temple (Wat Ling) locally known as monkey temple.
    •   Canoeing or Bamboo along the Sok River, one of the best way to explore this rich tropical rainforest thriving and enjoy the scenery limestone mountain.
    •   Make fresh-best coffee with bamboo cup in the jungle. Back to be a child use liana vine swing out jumping and swim like a Tarzan!.
    •   Enjoy lunch with Thai set menu.
    •   Get a close up experience with the Asian Elephant. Give elephant a bath, a scrub and enjoy playing with them in the canal.
    •   Relaxing and feed elephant's favorite fruits.
    •   Transfer back to hotel.
